# Evaluation Questionnaire: Explainable Stock Movement Prediction Web App

**Target respondents**: Finance students, retail traders, ML/DS students, academic supervisors. **Aim**: assess usability, explainability, trust, actionability of the demo.

## Instructions
Rate each item 1-5 (1 = Strongly disagree/Very poor, 5 = Strongly agree/Very good).

## Section 1: Demographics
1. Role: Retail trader / Finance student / ML/DS student / Academic / Other
2. Trading experience: None / <1 yr / 1-3 yrs / >3 yrs
3. ML/XAI familiarity: None / Basic / Intermediate / Advanced

## Section 2: Usability & Interface (Q1-Q5)
Q1. Interface is clear and easy to navigate (stock/model/date selection)
Q2. Information is well-organized and readable
Q3. App is responsive when viewing explanations
Q4. Visuals (SHAP bar chart, probabilities) are easy to interpret
Q5. I could use this app without technical support

## Section 3: Explainability & Understanding (Q6-Q11)
Q6. Prediction + probability + baseline are understandable
Q7. Top features and their direction (push UP/DOWN) are clear
Q8. SHAP bar chart helps explain why prediction was made
Q9. Natural-language explanation (Gemini/cached) clarifies the decision
Q10. Explanations help distinguish correct vs wrong predictions
Q11. I understand the 'chance-level' disclaimer (~50% accuracy)

## Section 4: Trust & Credibility (Q12-Q16)
Q12. Chance-level caveat is honest and appropriate
Q13. Showing wrong predictions builds trustworthiness
Q14. Local explanations feel faithful to shown features
Q15. Presented clearly as explanatory demo, not trading advice
Q16. Transparency about limitations increases credibility

## Section 5: Actionability & Usefulness (Q17-Q22)
Q17. Helps think critically about technical signals
Q18. Helps spot when signals are weak/noisy
Q19. Discourages over-relying on single indicators
Q20. Useful for educational purposes
Q21. Useful for research purposes
Q22. I would use a similar tool to evaluate trading ideas

## Section 6: Overall (Q23-Q25)
Q23. Overall usefulness for learning about XAI in trading (1-5)
Q24. Overall clarity of explanations (1-5)
Q25. Likelihood to recommend to others interested in XAI/trading (1-5)
